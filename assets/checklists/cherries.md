# CHERRIES — Question Set (internet and web surveys)

Source: Checklist for Reporting Results of Internet E-Surveys, https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/cherries/ (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location or a reason.

This file paraphrases the CHERRIES items. Use it whenever data came from an
online questionnaire, including panel surveys and crowdsourced samples.

## 1. Design
**Question:** Is the survey design described?
**Guidance:** Cross-sectional, longitudinal, open or closed to a defined population.

## 2. IRB approval
**Question:** Was the study approved by an ethics committee, and is that stated?
**Guidance:** Name the body and the reference number.

## 3. Informed consent
**Question:** How was informed consent obtained, and what were participants told about length, data storage and the investigator?
**Guidance:** Include the consent text or a summary of it.

## 4. Data protection
**Question:** How were the data stored and protected?
**Guidance:** Where, for how long, and who can access them.

## 5. Development and testing
**Question:** Was the questionnaire pre-tested, and how?
**Guidance:** Usability testing, cognitive interviews, pilot round with a separate sample.

## 6. Open or closed survey
**Question:** Was the survey open to the public or restricted to an invited sample?
**Guidance:** This determines whether a response rate can be computed at all.

## 7. Contact mode
**Question:** How was the initial contact with the sample made?
**Guidance:** Email, panel, social media, offline recruitment to an online instrument.

## 8. Advertising the survey
**Question:** How was the survey advertised or promoted?
**Guidance:** Include the exact wording where it could bias who responded.

## 9. Web or email survey
**Question:** Was the survey delivered on a website or by email?
**Guidance:** Name the platform.

## 10. Context
**Question:** On what type of site was the survey hosted, and what was its context?
**Guidance:** A survey on a patient forum draws a different sample than one on a university page.

## 11. Mandatory items
**Question:** Were any items mandatory, and could participants skip questions?
**Guidance:** Forced responses change both completion and honesty.

## 12. Number of items and pages
**Question:** How many items and how many pages did the questionnaire contain?
**Guidance:** Report the structure, and include the instrument as an appendix.

## 13. Completeness check and review step
**Question:** Was completeness checked before submission, and could participants review and change answers?
**Guidance:** Back button behaviour matters for interpretation.

## 14. Incentives
**Question:** Were incentives offered, and of what kind and value?
**Guidance:** Incentives shape who responds.

## 15. Time and date stamp
**Question:** In what time frame was the survey conducted?
**Guidance:** Start and end dates.

## 16. Randomization and adaptive questioning
**Question:** Were items or blocks randomised, and was adaptive questioning used?
**Guidance:** Say which items were conditionally displayed.

## 17. Unique site visitor identification
**Question:** How were unique visitors identified, and how were duplicate entries prevented?
**Guidance:** Cookies, IP address, log file analysis, registration.

## 18. View rate and participation rate
**Question:** Are the ratios of unique visitors to first survey page, and of those who agreed to participate, reported?
**Guidance:** Define the denominator you used.

## 19. Completion rate
**Question:** What proportion of those who started finished the survey?
**Guidance:** Distinguish completion rate from participation rate.

## 20. Preventing multiple entries
**Question:** What methods prevented one person from responding more than once?
**Guidance:** Also say what was done with detected duplicates.

## 21. Analysis of incomplete questionnaires
**Question:** Were incomplete questionnaires analysed, and from what cutoff?
**Guidance:** State the inclusion rule before looking at the results.

## 22. Statistical correction
**Question:** Were any weighting or statistical corrections applied to adjust for the non-representative sample?
**Guidance:** Name the weights and their source population.
