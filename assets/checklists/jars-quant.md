# APA JARS-Quant — Question Set (quantitative psychology and education research)

Source: APA Journal Article Reporting Standards, https://apastyle.apa.org/jars (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location or a reason.

This file paraphrases the JARS-Quant common items. Design-specific modules
exist for experiments, quasi-experiments, longitudinal and replication studies;
consult the source for the module matching the design.

## 1. Title and author note
**Question:** Does the title identify the variables and the population, and does the author note carry funding, conflicts and acknowledgements?
**Guidance:** Also record any prior dissemination of the data.

## 2. Abstract
**Question:** Does the abstract state the problem, participants, method, findings with effect sizes, and conclusions?
**Guidance:** Include the registration status.

## 3. Problem and hypotheses
**Question:** Is the problem stated, and are hypotheses presented as primary, secondary or exploratory?
**Guidance:** Every hypothesis needs its own label; do not relabel after seeing results.

## 4. Participant characteristics
**Question:** Are the eligibility criteria and the major demographic characteristics reported?
**Guidance:** Report the characteristics that bear on generalisation, and say why those.

## 5. Sampling procedures
**Question:** Are the sampling method, settings, agreements and payments, and ethical oversight described?
**Guidance:** Name the IRB and the protocol number.

## 6. Sample size and power
**Question:** Is the intended sample size, the achieved sample size, and the basis for it reported?
**Guidance:** An a priori power analysis with the assumed effect size, or a stopping rule.

## 7. Measures and covariates
**Question:** Are all measures defined, with reliability and validity evidence for this sample?
**Guidance:** Report reliability computed on your own data, not only from the manual.

## 8. Data collection
**Question:** Are the data collection methods and the conditions described in enough detail to replicate?
**Guidance:** Include instructions, materials, and delivery mode.

## 9. Conditions and design
**Question:** Are the experimental conditions or design described, including assignment and blinding?
**Guidance:** NA for purely observational designs.

## 10. Data diagnostics
**Question:** Are missing data, outliers, distributional assumptions and exclusions reported, with the rules that produced them?
**Guidance:** State the exclusion rule and how many cases it removed.

## 11. Analytic strategy
**Question:** Is the analytic strategy described for primary, secondary and exploratory hypotheses?
**Guidance:** Report the software and version.

## 12. Results for each hypothesis
**Question:** For each hypothesis, are the results reported with effect sizes and confidence intervals?
**Guidance:** Exact p-values, not thresholds alone.

## 13. Support for conclusions
**Question:** Do the conclusions follow from the analyses that were run?
**Guidance:** Causal language requires a design that supports it.

## 14. Limitations and generalisability
**Question:** Are limitations, sources of bias and the boundaries of generalisation discussed?
**Guidance:** Say which population the result applies to.

## 15. Open practices
**Question:** Are the data, code, materials and preregistration available, and where?
**Guidance:** Give links. If unavailable, say why.
