# COREQ — Question Set (qualitative interview and focus group studies)

Source: COREQ 32-item checklist, https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/coreq/ (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location or a reason.

This file paraphrases the 32 COREQ items, grouped into the guideline's three
domains. Use it when data come from interviews or focus groups.

## 1. Interviewer identity
**Question:** Which author conducted the interviews or focus groups?
**Guidance:** Name them by initials in the manuscript.

## 2. Credentials
**Question:** What were the researcher's credentials at the time of the study?
**Guidance:** Degree and role, not a biography.

## 3. Occupation
**Question:** What was their occupation at the time of the study?
**Guidance:** Clinician, student, staff researcher.

## 4. Gender
**Question:** Is the researcher's gender reported?
**Guidance:** Report it because it can shape what participants say.

## 5. Experience and training
**Question:** What experience or training in qualitative methods did the researcher have?
**Guidance:** Prior studies, formal training, supervision.

## 6. Relationship established
**Question:** Was a relationship established before study commencement?
**Guidance:** Say whether participants already knew the researcher.

## 7. Participant knowledge of the interviewer
**Question:** What did participants know about the researcher and the research goals?
**Guidance:** Personal motivations and reasons for doing the research.

## 8. Interviewer characteristics
**Question:** What characteristics of the interviewer were reported, including assumptions and interests in the topic?
**Guidance:** This is the positionality statement.

## 9. Methodological orientation
**Question:** What methodological orientation underpinned the study?
**Guidance:** Grounded theory, phenomenology, thematic analysis, content analysis, discourse analysis.

## 10. Sampling
**Question:** How were participants selected?
**Guidance:** Purposive, convenience, consecutive, snowball. Say what the sampling logic served.

## 11. Method of approach
**Question:** How were participants approached?
**Guidance:** Face to face, telephone, mail, email.

## 12. Sample size
**Question:** How many participants were in the study?
**Guidance:** Report the number, and how the number was decided.

## 13. Non-participation
**Question:** How many people refused to participate or dropped out, and why?
**Guidance:** Give reasons where known.

## 14. Setting of data collection
**Question:** Where were the data collected?
**Guidance:** Home, clinic, workplace, online.

## 15. Presence of non-participants
**Question:** Was anyone else present besides the participants and researchers?
**Guidance:** Family members, interpreters, clinicians.

## 16. Description of sample
**Question:** What are the important characteristics of the sample?
**Guidance:** Demographic data relevant to the research question.

## 17. Interview guide
**Question:** Were questions, prompts or guides provided by the authors, and was the guide pilot tested?
**Guidance:** Include the guide as an appendix.

## 18. Repeat interviews
**Question:** Were repeat interviews carried out, and if so how many?
**Guidance:** NA if none.

## 19. Audio or visual recording
**Question:** Did the research use audio or visual recording to collect the data?
**Guidance:** If not, say how the record was made.

## 20. Field notes
**Question:** Were field notes made during or after the interview or focus group?
**Guidance:** Say who made them and how they were used in analysis.

## 21. Duration
**Question:** What was the duration of the interviews or focus groups?
**Guidance:** Range and mean.

## 22. Data saturation
**Question:** Was data saturation discussed?
**Guidance:** If claimed, say how it was judged. Saturation is not the only defensible stopping rule; say which one you used.

## 23. Transcripts returned
**Question:** Were transcripts returned to participants for comment or correction?
**Guidance:** NA is acceptable; state it.

## 24. Number of data coders
**Question:** How many data coders coded the data?
**Guidance:** Name their roles.

## 25. Description of the coding tree
**Question:** Did authors provide a description of the coding tree?
**Guidance:** A figure or appendix showing how codes nest into themes.

## 26. Derivation of themes
**Question:** Were themes identified in advance or derived from the data?
**Guidance:** Deductive, inductive, or both. Say which.

## 27. Software
**Question:** What software, if any, was used to manage the data?
**Guidance:** Name and version, or state that coding was manual.

## 28. Participant checking
**Question:** Did participants provide feedback on the findings?
**Guidance:** Member checking, respondent validation, or neither.

## 29. Quotations presented
**Question:** Are participant quotations presented to illustrate the themes, and are they identified?
**Guidance:** Use consistent pseudonymous identifiers.

## 30. Data and findings consistent
**Question:** Was there consistency between the data presented and the findings?
**Guidance:** Each theme should be traceable to quoted data.

## 31. Clarity of major themes
**Question:** Were the major themes clearly presented in the findings?
**Guidance:** Named, defined, and distinguishable from each other.

## 32. Clarity of minor themes
**Question:** Is there a description of diverse cases or discussion of minor themes?
**Guidance:** Report the cases that did not fit.
