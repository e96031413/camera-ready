Source: https://neurips.cc/public/guides/PaperChecklist (fetched 2026-07-30).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Justifications are encouraged (point to the
section where supporting material appears) but the official guide does not

## 1. Claims
**Question:** Do the main claims made in the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?
**Guidance:** Match claims to what experiments/theory actually support; don't oversell.

## 2. Limitations
**Question:** Does the paper discuss the limitations of the work?
**Guidance:** A dedicated Limitations section or subsection is the safest way to satisfy this.

## 3. Theory Assumptions and Proofs
**Question:** For each theoretical result, does the paper state the full set of assumptions and include a complete proof?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper has no theoretical results.

## 4. Experimental Result Reproducibility
**Question:** Does the paper disclose all information needed to reproduce the main experimental results?
**Guidance:** Cover data, model, training/eval procedure, and any code released.

## 5. Open Access to Data and Code
**Question:** Does the paper provide open access to data and code with sufficient instructions to reproduce the main results?
**Guidance:** An anonymized repository link or a statement of released assets satisfies this.

## 6. Experimental Setting/Details
**Question:** Does the paper specify all training and test details (data splits, hyperparameters, how they were chosen, type of optimizer, etc.) needed to understand the results?
**Guidance:** Usually satisfied by an Experimental Setup section or appendix.

## 7. Experiment Statistical Significance
**Question:** Does the paper report error bars, confidence intervals, or other statistical significance information for the main experiments?
**Guidance:** Multiple seeds + stderr/CI is the common bar; a single run without variance reporting should answer No with justification.

## 8. Experiments Compute Resources
**Question:** Does the paper provide sufficient information on the compute resources (type of compute, memory, execution time) needed to reproduce the experiments?
**Guidance:** GPU/TPU type, count, and approximate runtime is typically enough.

## 9. Code Of Ethics
**Question:** Has the research been conducted in conformance with the NeurIPS Code of Ethics?
**Guidance:** Cannot be inferred automatically — the author must confirm this.

## 10. Broader Impacts
**Question:** Does the paper discuss both potential positive and negative societal impacts of the work?
**Guidance:** NA is acceptable if the work is foundational/methodological with no direct societal application.

## 11. Safeguards
**Question:** Does the paper describe safeguards for responsible release of high-risk models or data (if any are released)?
**Guidance:** NA if no such high-risk assets are released.

## 12. Licenses for Existing Assets
**Question:** Are existing assets (code, data, models) used in the paper properly credited, with license and terms of use respected and stated?
**Guidance:** Cannot be inferred automatically — the author must confirm licenses were checked.

## 13. New Assets
**Question:** Are new assets introduced in the paper (code, data, models) well documented, and is the documentation provided alongside the release?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper releases no new assets.

## 14. Crowdsourcing and Research with Human Subjects
**Question:** For crowdsourcing experiments and research with human subjects, does the paper include full instructions and compensation details given to participants?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper involves no human subjects/crowdsourcing.

## 15. IRB Approvals
**Question:** For research with human subjects, does the paper describe whether IRB approvals (or equivalent) were obtained?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper involves no human subjects research.

## 16. Declaration of LLM Usage
**Question:** If an LLM is used as an important, original, or non-standard component of the core methods, is its usage described?
**Guidance:** Not required if an LLM was only used for writing/editing/formatting and did not affect the core methodology.
