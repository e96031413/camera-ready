# CONSORT 2010 — Question Set (randomized controlled trials)

Source: CONSORT 2010 statement, https://www.equator-network.org/reporting-guidelines/consort/ (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location or a reason.

This file paraphrases the CONSORT items. The guideline numbers 25 items, several
of which have lettered parts; each part is listed here as its own row, giving 37
rows in total. Journals that require CONSORT want
the official checklist and flow diagram submitted with the manuscript.

Use this for parallel-group randomized trials. Extensions exist for cluster,
crossover, pilot and non-inferiority designs; check the source for the right one.

## 1a. Title
**Question:** Does the title identify the study as a randomized trial?
**Guidance:** The word "randomized" belongs in the title.

## 1b. Structured abstract
**Question:** Does the abstract give a structured summary of design, methods, results and conclusions?
**Guidance:** Follow the CONSORT for Abstracts item list.

## 2a. Background
**Question:** Is the scientific background and rationale explained?
**Guidance:** Say why the comparison is in genuine equipoise.

## 2b. Objectives
**Question:** Are specific objectives or hypotheses stated?
**Guidance:** Distinguish the primary from the secondary.

## 3a. Trial design
**Question:** Is the design described, including allocation ratio?
**Guidance:** Parallel, factorial, crossover; superiority, equivalence, non-inferiority.

## 3b. Changes to design
**Question:** Are any changes to methods after trial commencement reported, with reasons?
**Guidance:** Including changes to eligibility criteria.

## 4a. Participants
**Question:** Are eligibility criteria for participants given?
**Guidance:** Both inclusion and exclusion.

## 4b. Settings
**Question:** Are the settings and locations where the data were collected given?
**Guidance:** Country, type of centre, number of sites.

## 5. Interventions
**Question:** Are the interventions for each group described in enough detail to allow replication, including how and when they were administered?
**Guidance:** Dose, schedule, delivery, provider training, adherence monitoring.

## 6a. Outcomes
**Question:** Are primary and secondary outcome measures completely defined, including when they were assessed?
**Guidance:** Instrument, scale range, direction of benefit, time points.

## 6b. Changes to outcomes
**Question:** Are any changes to trial outcomes after the trial started reported, with reasons?
**Guidance:** Compare against the registered protocol and say what moved.

## 7a. Sample size
**Question:** How was sample size determined?
**Guidance:** Effect size assumed, power, alpha, expected attrition.

## 7b. Interim analyses
**Question:** Are interim analyses and stopping guidelines explained?
**Guidance:** NA if none were planned; say so.

## 8a. Sequence generation
**Question:** What method was used to generate the random allocation sequence?
**Guidance:** Name the mechanism; "randomly assigned" is not a method.

## 8b. Randomization type
**Question:** Is the type of randomization and any restriction described?
**Guidance:** Blocking, block size, stratification factors.

## 9. Allocation concealment
**Question:** What mechanism implemented the allocation sequence, and how was it concealed until assignment?
**Guidance:** Central randomization, sequentially numbered opaque sealed envelopes.

## 10. Implementation
**Question:** Who generated the sequence, who enrolled participants, and who assigned them?
**Guidance:** These should not all be the same person.

## 11a. Blinding
**Question:** Who was blinded after assignment, and how?
**Guidance:** Participants, providers, outcome assessors, analysts.

## 11b. Similarity of interventions
**Question:** If relevant, is the similarity of the interventions described?
**Guidance:** Placebo appearance, sham procedures.

## 12a. Statistical methods
**Question:** Which statistical methods compared groups on the primary and secondary outcomes?
**Guidance:** Name the model, the handling of clustering, and the software version.

## 12b. Additional analyses
**Question:** Are methods for subgroup and adjusted analyses given?
**Guidance:** State which were prespecified and which are exploratory.

## 13a. Participant flow
**Question:** Are the numbers randomly assigned, receiving intended treatment, and analysed for the primary outcome reported for each group?
**Guidance:** A CONSORT flow diagram is expected.

## 13b. Losses and exclusions
**Question:** Are losses and exclusions after randomization reported for each group, with reasons?
**Guidance:** Numbers must reconcile with the flow diagram.

## 14a. Recruitment
**Question:** Are the dates defining the recruitment and follow-up periods given?
**Guidance:** Actual dates, not durations alone.

## 14b. Trial stoppage
**Question:** Why did the trial end or stop?
**Guidance:** Reached target, stopped early for harm or futility, funding.

## 15. Baseline data
**Question:** Is a table of baseline demographic and clinical characteristics given for each group?
**Guidance:** Do not significance-test baseline differences.

## 16. Numbers analysed
**Question:** For each group, is the number included in each analysis stated, and was the analysis by original assigned groups?
**Guidance:** Intention-to-treat, per-protocol, or both; say which is primary.

## 17a. Outcomes and estimation
**Question:** For each outcome, are results for each group and the estimated effect size with precision reported?
**Guidance:** Confidence intervals, not p-values alone.

## 17b. Binary outcomes
**Question:** For binary outcomes, are both absolute and relative effect sizes presented?
**Guidance:** Risk difference alongside risk ratio.

## 18. Ancillary analyses
**Question:** Are other analyses reported, distinguishing prespecified from exploratory?
**Guidance:** Including subgroup analyses.

## 19. Harms
**Question:** Are all important harms or unintended effects in each group reported?
**Guidance:** Follow the CONSORT harms extension. Absence of reporting is not absence of harm.

## 20. Limitations
**Question:** Are trial limitations addressed, including sources of potential bias and imprecision?
**Guidance:** Also address multiplicity if many outcomes were analysed.

## 21. Generalisability
**Question:** Is the external validity of the findings discussed?
**Guidance:** Say to which population and setting the result applies.

## 22. Interpretation
**Question:** Is the interpretation consistent with results, balancing benefits and harms, and considering other evidence?
**Guidance:** A non-significant result is not evidence of equivalence.

## 23. Registration
**Question:** Is the registration number and the name of the trial registry given?
**Guidance:** Registration must be prospective.

## 24. Protocol
**Question:** Is the full trial protocol available, and where?
**Guidance:** A link or a statement that it is available on request.

## 25. Funding
**Question:** Are sources of funding and other support named, with the role of funders?
**Guidance:** State the funder's role in design, analysis and the decision to publish.
