# ICLR Paper Checklist — Question Set

Source: ICLR author guide, <https://iclr.cc/Conferences/2026/AuthorGuide>.
Answer options: Yes / No / NA.

**Before submission**: re-check this file's wording against the current
`iclr20XX_conference.sty` and the official author guide. ICLR's requirements
around LLM disclosure, reproducibility, and ethics statements have changed
several times. This file **paraphrases** the official topics; it is not a
verbatim copy.

ICLR treats three things as statements in the paper rather than as checklist
items alone: the **Reproducibility Statement**, the **Ethics Statement**, and
the **LLM usage disclosure**. Answering the questions below does not remove
the need to write those sections.

## 1. Claims
**Question:** Do the abstract and introduction accurately reflect the paper's contributions and scope?
**Guidance:** Reviewers check the abstract against the results table first. Do not promise what Section 5 does not deliver.

## 2. Limitations
**Question:** Does the paper discuss its limitations and the conditions under which the results may not hold?
**Guidance:** A dedicated Limitations section or subsection is the safest way to satisfy this.

## 3. Theory Assumptions and Proofs
**Question:** For every theoretical claim, are the assumptions stated in full and is a complete proof provided?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper contains no theoretical results.

## 4. Reproducibility Statement
**Question:** Does the paper contain a Reproducibility Statement pointing to where the reader can find the information needed to reproduce the results?
**Guidance:** ICLR expects a short, separate statement that links to the relevant sections, appendices, and supplementary material. It does not count toward the page limit.

## 5. Open Access to Code and Data
**Question:** Are code and data available, or is their absence explained?
**Guidance:** Use an anonymized repository at submission time. A named personal or lab repository breaks double-blind review.

## 6. Experimental Settings and Details
**Question:** Are data splits, hyperparameters, hyperparameter selection procedure, and optimizer settings specified?
**Guidance:** Appendix placement is acceptable; a pointer from the main text is expected.

## 7. Statistical Significance
**Question:** Are error bars, confidence intervals, or other variability measures reported for the main results?
**Guidance:** State what the interval represents (seeds, data splits, bootstrap) — an unlabelled shaded region is not enough.

## 8. Compute Resources
**Question:** Does the paper describe the compute needed to reproduce the experiments?
**Guidance:** Hardware type, count, and wall-clock time. Include the exploration cost, not only the final runs.

## 9. Ethics Statement
**Question:** Does the paper include an Ethics Statement covering conflicts of interest, data privacy, human subjects, fairness, and potential harms where applicable?
**Guidance:** ICLR asks for this as a separate statement that does not count toward the page limit. Write "The authors declare no ethical concerns" only if that is genuinely true after considering each category.

## 10. Responsible Release
**Question:** For released high-risk models or datasets, does the paper describe the safeguards?
**Guidance:** NA if nothing high-risk is released.

## 11. Licenses and Credit for Existing Assets
**Question:** Are third-party code, data, and models cited, with their licenses and terms of use respected and stated?
**Guidance:** Cannot be inferred automatically — the author must confirm this.

## 12. New Assets
**Question:** Are new assets documented, with documentation provided at release?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper releases no new assets.

## 13. Human Subjects and Crowdsourcing
**Question:** For work involving human participants, does the paper report the instructions, compensation, and consent procedure?
**Guidance:** NA if the paper involves no human subjects.

## 14. Anonymity
**Question:** Does the submission comply with double-blind review — no author names, no acknowledgements, no identifying repository or institution URLs?
**Guidance:** Run `python scripts/anonymity_check.py --project-dir <paper_dir>` before answering. A tool pass is evidence, not proof; read the flagged lines yourself.

## 15. LLM Usage Disclosure
**Question:** Does the paper disclose the use of large language models where that use goes beyond routine writing assistance?
**Guidance:** ICLR requires disclosure when an LLM contributed to ideation, experiment design, analysis, or the method itself. Ordinary grammar and copy-editing help does not require disclosure, but the authors remain responsible for every sentence in the paper.
