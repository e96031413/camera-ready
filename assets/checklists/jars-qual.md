# APA JARS-Qual — Question Set (qualitative psychology and education research)

Source: APA Journal Article Reporting Standards for qualitative research, https://apastyle.apa.org/jars (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location or a reason.

This file paraphrases the JARS-Qual common items. For mixed methods work,
answer this alongside jars-quant rather than instead of it.

## 1. Title and author note
**Question:** Does the title convey the qualitative approach and the phenomenon studied?
**Guidance:** Name the method in the title or the abstract.

## 2. Abstract
**Question:** Does the abstract describe the problem, participants, approach, findings and implications?
**Guidance:** Avoid quantitative framing of qualitative findings.

## 3. Description of the problem
**Question:** Is the problem framed in a way that shows why a qualitative approach fits?
**Guidance:** State the gap in understanding, not the gap in measurement.

## 4. Study objectives
**Question:** Are the study aims or questions stated, and did they shift during the study?
**Guidance:** Emergent designs change; report the change.

## 5. Research design and approach
**Question:** Is the design and its methodological grounding described?
**Guidance:** Name the tradition and cite its methodological literature.

## 6. Researcher description
**Question:** Are the researchers' backgrounds, perspectives and relationships to participants described?
**Guidance:** Include how these were managed during analysis.

## 7. Participants and data sources
**Question:** Are participants or data sources described, with the recruitment and selection process?
**Guidance:** Report the number and the rationale for it.

## 8. Ethics
**Question:** Are ethics approval, informed consent and confidentiality protections reported?
**Guidance:** Say how identifying detail was removed from quotations.

## 9. Data collection
**Question:** Are the data collection procedures and their evolution described?
**Guidance:** Include the interview guide or observation protocol.

## 10. Data analysis
**Question:** Is the analytic process described in enough detail to be followed?
**Guidance:** Coding, categorising, theme development, and who did each step.

## 11. Methodological integrity
**Question:** Is the fidelity to the subject matter and the utility of the findings substantiated?
**Guidance:** Address adequacy of data, perspective management, groundedness and coherence.

## 12. Findings
**Question:** Are the findings presented with supporting evidence from the data?
**Guidance:** Quotations attributed to pseudonymous identifiers.

## 13. Discussion
**Question:** Are the findings related to prior work, with implications and limits of transferability?
**Guidance:** Do not claim statistical generalisation.

## 14. Open practices
**Question:** Are materials, and any shareable data, available, and where?
**Guidance:** Qualitative data often cannot be shared; say so and say why.
