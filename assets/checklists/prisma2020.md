# PRISMA 2020 — Question Set (systematic reviews and meta-analyses)

Source: PRISMA 2020 statement, https://www.prisma-statement.org/ (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location (section, page, or
figure) or a one-line reason for NA.

This file paraphrases the 27 PRISMA 2020 items so the workflow can generate a
worksheet. It is not the official checklist. Journals that require PRISMA want
the official checklist file submitted alongside the manuscript; download it
from the source above and fill it in against the finished paper.

Use this when the paper synthesises results across studies: systematic review,
meta-analysis, scoping review with a formal search, or rapid review.

## 1. Title
**Question:** Does the title identify the report as a systematic review?
**Guidance:** The word "systematic review" or "meta-analysis" belongs in the title itself.

## 2. Abstract
**Question:** Does the abstract follow the PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts checklist?
**Guidance:** Objectives, eligibility, sources, risk of bias, synthesis method, results with effect estimates, limitations, registration.

## 3. Rationale
**Question:** Does the introduction explain why the review is needed against what is already known?
**Guidance:** Cite the existing reviews and say what they left unresolved.

## 4. Objectives
**Question:** Is there an explicit statement of the questions the review addresses?
**Guidance:** State them as PICO or an equivalent structured framing.

## 5. Eligibility criteria
**Question:** Are the inclusion and exclusion criteria stated, and are the grouping rules for synthesis given?
**Guidance:** Criteria must be decidable by a second reader without asking you.

## 6. Information sources
**Question:** Are all databases, registers, websites and other sources named, with the last search date for each?
**Guidance:** Include grey literature and citation chasing if used; give the date, not just the year.

## 7. Search strategy
**Question:** Is the full search strategy presented for at least one database, including every filter and limit?
**Guidance:** A reproducible query string, usually in an appendix. Saying "we searched PubMed" is not a strategy.

## 8. Selection process
**Question:** How many reviewers screened each record, did they work independently, and what tools assisted them?
**Guidance:** Report the disagreement resolution rule and any automation.

## 9. Data collection process
**Question:** How were data extracted, by how many reviewers, and how were study authors contacted?
**Guidance:** Same independence and resolution reporting as screening.

## 10. Data items
**Question:** Are all outcomes and all other variables sought from each study listed, with the rules for choosing among multiple measures?
**Guidance:** State what you did when a study reported several time points or instruments.

## 11. Study risk of bias assessment
**Question:** Which tool assessed risk of bias, applied by how many reviewers, working how?
**Guidance:** Name the tool (RoB 2, ROBINS-I, QUADAS-2, Newcastle-Ottawa) and its version.

## 12. Effect measures
**Question:** Which effect measure was used for each outcome?
**Guidance:** Risk ratio, odds ratio, mean difference, standardised mean difference, hazard ratio.

## 13. Synthesis methods
**Question:** Are the synthesis methods described: eligibility for each synthesis, data preparation, models, heterogeneity, sensitivity analyses?
**Guidance:** Name the model (fixed or random effects), the estimator, and the software with version.

## 14. Reporting bias assessment
**Question:** How was risk of bias due to missing results assessed?
**Guidance:** Funnel plots and asymmetry tests need a stated minimum number of studies.

## 15. Certainty assessment
**Question:** How was certainty in the body of evidence assessed?
**Guidance:** Usually GRADE; report the domains that were downgraded and why.

## 16. Study selection results
**Question:** Is there a flow diagram giving records identified, screened, excluded and included, with reasons for exclusion at full text?
**Guidance:** The PRISMA flow diagram is expected; numbers must reconcile.

## 17. Study characteristics
**Question:** Are the characteristics of each included study presented?
**Guidance:** Usually a table: design, population, intervention, comparator, outcomes, setting.

## 18. Risk of bias in studies
**Question:** Are risk of bias assessments presented for each included study?
**Guidance:** Per study and per domain, not a single overall verdict.

## 19. Results of individual studies
**Question:** For every outcome, are each study's summary statistics and effect estimate with precision reported?
**Guidance:** Forest plots satisfy this when they carry the numbers.

## 20. Results of syntheses
**Question:** Are the synthesis results reported, with the studies contributing, the summary estimate, precision, and heterogeneity?
**Guidance:** Also report subgroup and sensitivity analyses and say which were preplanned.

## 21. Reporting biases
**Question:** Are the assessments of risk of bias due to missing results reported?
**Guidance:** State the conclusion, not just that the test was run.

## 22. Certainty of evidence
**Question:** Is the certainty in the body of evidence reported for each outcome?
**Guidance:** A summary of findings table is the usual form.

## 23. Discussion
**Question:** Does the discussion interpret the results in context and state limitations of the evidence and of the review process?
**Guidance:** Separate the limitations of the included evidence from the limitations of your method.

## 24. Registration and protocol
**Question:** Is the review registered, is the registration number given, and is the protocol available?
**Guidance:** PROSPERO or an equivalent. If not registered, say so explicitly. Report amendments to the protocol.

## 25. Support
**Question:** Are the sources of financial and non-financial support named, with the role of the funders?
**Guidance:** State explicitly if there was no funding.

## 26. Competing interests
**Question:** Are competing interests of all authors declared?
**Guidance:** State explicitly if there are none.

## 27. Availability of data, code and other materials
**Question:** Are the extraction template, extracted data, and analytic code available, and where?
**Guidance:** Give a repository link and a licence, or say what is not available and why.
