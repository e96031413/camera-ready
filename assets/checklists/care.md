# CARE — Question Set (clinical case reports)

Source: CARE guidelines, https://www.care-statement.org/ (checked 2026-09-05).
Answer options: Yes / No / NA. Every answer needs a location or a reason.

This file paraphrases the 13 CARE items. Use it for single case reports and
small case series.

## 1. Title
**Question:** Does the title contain the words "case report" and name the phenomenon of greatest interest?
**Guidance:** Symptom, diagnosis, test, intervention or outcome.

## 2. Keywords
**Question:** Are 2 to 5 keywords given that identify the areas covered?
**Guidance:** Include the primary diagnosis.

## 3. Abstract
**Question:** Does the abstract cover what is unusual about this case, the main symptoms and findings, the interventions and outcomes, and the takeaway lesson?
**Guidance:** Structured or unstructured, per the journal.

## 4. Introduction
**Question:** Does the introduction summarise why this case is unique, with references?
**Guidance:** Say what the literature already contains.

## 5. Patient information
**Question:** Are de-identified demographics, main concerns and symptoms, and relevant medical, family and psychosocial history given?
**Guidance:** Include relevant past interventions and their outcomes. Remove every direct identifier.

## 6. Clinical findings
**Question:** Are the relevant physical examination findings described?
**Guidance:** Report what was normal as well as what was not, where it matters.

## 7. Timeline
**Question:** Is there a timeline of episodes of care?
**Guidance:** A figure or table with dates relative to presentation.

## 8. Diagnostic assessment
**Question:** Are diagnostic methods, challenges, differential diagnoses and prognostic characteristics reported?
**Guidance:** Include tests that were considered and not done, and why.

## 9. Therapeutic intervention
**Question:** Are the types of intervention, their administration and any changes reported?
**Guidance:** Doses, routes, durations, and reasons for change.

## 10. Follow-up and outcomes
**Question:** Are clinician- and patient-assessed outcomes, adverse events and follow-up test results reported?
**Guidance:** Give the length of follow-up.

## 11. Discussion
**Question:** Are the strengths and limitations of managing this case discussed against the relevant literature?
**Guidance:** Include the rationale for the conclusions and the main lesson.

## 12. Patient perspective
**Question:** Does the patient share their perspective on their experience where possible?
**Guidance:** NA is acceptable, but the guideline asks for it.

## 13. Informed consent
**Question:** Did the patient give informed consent, and is that stated?
**Guidance:** Almost all journals require this. Provide it on request.
